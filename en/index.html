Punimoru Support

Thank you for playing “Punimoru ~ Rainy-Day Bath Cleaning ~”.
Squishy little sprites called “moru” pop up in the bath during the rainy season — scrub them with your sponge and gently send them off in this cozy, healing cleaning game.

How to play

  • Scrub the screen with the sponge to send off the moru.
  • One round lasts 15 seconds — perfect for a quick break.
  • Meet ordinary, boss, and hydrangea moru — plus seasonal visitors.
  • Every moru you send off is recorded in the Moru Book (the book icon on the title screen).
  • Clean for three days and you’ll receive a message from Mom.

FAQ

A moru won’t disappear
Keep scrubbing it with the sponge. Boss moru are a little tougher — a longer scrub will send them off.
No sound
Please check your device’s silent-mode switch and volume, as well as the in-app volume settings (gear icon).
About ads
The “Continue” video ad is optional — watching it grants rewards such as extra time. You can enjoy the game without watching any ads.
How do I delete my data?
Game data is stored only on your device. Deleting the app removes all data.
