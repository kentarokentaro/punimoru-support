Punimoru Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2, 2026

Introduction

“Punimoru ~ Rainy-Day Bath Cleaning ~” (the “App”) respects your privacy. This policy explains what information the App handles.

Information the App collects directly

The App does not directly collect personally identifiable information such as your name, email address, phone number, or postal address. Game progress data (settings such as volume, the three-day cleaning record, the total number of moru sent off, whether the intro has been shown, etc.) is stored only on your device (UserDefaults) and is never sent to the developer’s servers.

Information handled by third-party services

Google AdMob (advertising)

The App serves rewarded ads (optional video ads that grant in-game rewards) through Google AdMob. AdMob may access device information such as the advertising identifier (IDFA) for ad delivery and measurement. For details, please see the Google Privacy Policy.

The App is a general-audience app for all ages. If you grant the tracking permission described below (ATT), you may see interest-based (personalized) ads. If you do not, ads are still shown but are not personalized.

Open-Meteo (weather)

For a seasonal touch (humidity affects how many moru appear), the App fetches the current humidity from the public Open-Meteo API. It does not use the location (GPS) permission. It only makes a rough regional guess from your device’s time zone (e.g. Asia/Tokyo) and sends no personally identifying information. If the weather cannot be fetched, the game plays normally at the default difficulty.

App Tracking Transparency (ATT)

On first launch (when the title screen appears), the App shows a dialog asking for tracking permission. You are free to allow or deny it, and you can change your choice at any time in the iOS Settings app. The App is fully playable either way.

For users in the European Economic Area (EEA) and the United Kingdom

In the EEA, the UK, and Switzerland, a consent form provided by Google’s consent management platform (UMP) is shown before ads are served. You can make your choices on the form at any time, and the game remains fully playable even if you do not consent (ad personalization will be limited).

Children’s privacy

The App does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and have any concerns, please contact us below.

Data storage and deletion

All game data is stored in your device’s local storage. Deleting the App erases this data.
